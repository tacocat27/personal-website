Marina Yu

I’m Marina, an undergraduate at Stanford pursuing a B.A. in Philosophy and a B.S. in Mathematics.

Broadly, I’m curious about the mechanisms underlying the world, whether that be systems, organizations, markets, or people. More specifically, I’m drawn to why things work the way they do, what assumptions we make, and what happens when we challenge them.

In my free time, you can usually find me somewhere in the mountains or immersed in music.

I’m maintaining this website as a place to track how my thoughts change over time.
